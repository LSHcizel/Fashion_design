# Schiaparelli Spring/Summer 2026 schiaparelli_ss26_satin_chain_gown

## text_description

A worn evening gown in the 1930s manner: glossy satin panels floating on a sheer base, with chain straps. The satin does not make a solid opaque dress; panels of gloss sit on sheer ground so both layers are visible, and the straps are chains. It is worn on the body, the sheer base and the satin panels moving together, the chains marking the shoulder line. The identity is that satin-on-sheer panel gown with chain straps, not a fully lined column and not a corseted hourglass.
