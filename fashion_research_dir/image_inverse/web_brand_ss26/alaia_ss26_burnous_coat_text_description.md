# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_burnous_coat

## text_description

A worn sweeping coat with the abstract air of a North African burnous, shown with the collection's easier shapes beside edge-to-edge jackets. The coat wraps and sweeps, loose rather than hourglass, with an enveloping outer line. It is worn open enough for the wrap to read, the long sweep of cloth as the silhouette. The identity is that burnous-like sweeping coat, not a fitted trench and not the fringe sculpture coat.
