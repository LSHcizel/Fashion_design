# Schiaparelli Spring/Summer 2026 schiaparelli_ss26_punched_leather_shirt

## text_description

A worn leather shirt punched with oversized holes. The shirt is leather, and the holes are large enough to read as a repeated opening through the material rather than small perforations. It is worn on the torso as a shirt, with the punched field visible across the leather. The identity is that hole-punched leather shirt; the openings have to stay part of the surface, not be filled back in as solid leather.
