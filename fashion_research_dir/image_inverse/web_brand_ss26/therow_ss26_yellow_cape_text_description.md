# The Row Spring/Summer 2026 therow_ss26_yellow_cape

## text_description

A worn yellow cape cut as sturdy as a Mackintosh. The cloth holds a firm, weather-coat body rather than a fluttering chiffon wrap, and the color is a clear yellow. It is worn over the shoulders as a cape, the solid yellow shell making one continuous outer shape. The identity is that jaunty, structured yellow cape; it should not collapse into a soft scarf or a tailored yellow blazer.
