# Schiaparelli Spring/Summer 2026 schiaparelli_ss26_pebble_tailoring

## text_description

A worn tailored look pared back to clean lines, then interrupted by pebble-shaped pockets and a curved waistband. The tailoring itself is reduced, without the house's usual gold hardware, and the off-kilter details sit on that quiet cloth: pockets rounded like pebbles, a waistband that curves instead of running straight. It is worn as a tailored garment on the body. The identity is those pebble pockets and the curved waistband on otherwise restrained tailoring.
