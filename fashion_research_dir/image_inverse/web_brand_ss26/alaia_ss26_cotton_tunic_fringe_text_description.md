# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_cotton_tunic_fringe

## text_description

A worn look opened by a pure, stark cotton tunic with a stand collar, paired with stockings whose tops release a cascade of silk fringe. The tunic is simple and collar-standing, cut as a short-coat-like cotton layer. Below it, silk fringe falls from the stocking tops and moves against the leg. The contrast is the identifying pair: a plain stand-collar cotton tunic above, and the cascading silk fringe at the stocking line. Both have to remain readable on the body.
