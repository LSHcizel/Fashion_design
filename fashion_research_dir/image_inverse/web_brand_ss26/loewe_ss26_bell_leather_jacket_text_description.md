# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_bell_leather_jacket

## text_description

A worn runway look centered on a glossy heat-sealed leather jacket sculpted into a bell. The leather reads smooth and sealed rather than matte, with a flared body that widens away from the torso and a crafted, molded volume through the hem. It is shown in the sunny yellow-and-red register of the collection's opening color note. The jacket is the identifying garment: a leather-house piece pushed into a bell-shaped outer shell, worn as a single high-craft layer over the body rather than as a flat tailored blazer.
