# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_backless_sweater_leggings

## text_description

A worn one-piece hybrid: a backless turtleneck continued into sweater-leggings. The neck is a turtleneck, the back is open, and the knit does not stop at the hip; it continues down the legs as leggings. It is worn as a single knitted body, with the open back and the legging extension both visible. The identity is that backless turtleneck-to-legging knit, not a separate sweater over separate tights.
