# The Row Spring/Summer 2026 therow_ss26_shantung_pajama_vest

## text_description

A worn look built as a vintage silk shantung pajama ensemble with a cashmere sweater vest. The pajama is silk shantung, so the surface has that dry, crisp sheen, cut as a matching relaxed set. The cashmere vest sits over it as a softer knit layer. Low pumps are worn with the pajama hem. The identity is the shantung pajama left visible under the cashmere vest, not a tailored suit and not a bare evening dress.
