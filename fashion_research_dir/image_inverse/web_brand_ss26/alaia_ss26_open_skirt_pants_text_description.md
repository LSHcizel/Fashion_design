# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_open_skirt_pants

## text_description

A worn hybrid lower garment: skirt-pants left open at the sides. The piece reads as both a skirt and a pant, with the side openings exposing the construction so the two forms are visible at once. It is worn on the body, the open sides marking a continuous edge from hip toward the hem. The identity is that open-sided skirt-pant, not a closed trouser and not a closed skirt.
