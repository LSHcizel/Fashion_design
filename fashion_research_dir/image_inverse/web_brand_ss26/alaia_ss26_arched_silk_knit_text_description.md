# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_arched_silk_knit

## text_description

A worn body-conscious look in seamless silk knit shaped into taut arches. The knit is held in place at the wrists and hooked into the heels of the shoes, so the garment is tensioned from hand to foot rather than ending in a free hem. The silk knit lies close to the body, seamless, with the arched spans as the silhouette. The identity is that continuous pulled knit: wrist anchors, heel hooks, and the arched line between them.
