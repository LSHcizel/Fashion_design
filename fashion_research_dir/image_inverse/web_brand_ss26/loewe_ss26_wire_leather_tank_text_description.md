# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_wire_leather_tank

## text_description

A worn tank or tee whose cloth is leather yarn shot through with wire. The wire holds the leather strands into a crinkled, irregular shape, so the top does not hang like jersey. It is worn close to the torso as a simple tank or T-shirt silhouette, but the surface buckles and crisps where the wire runs through the leather yarn. That crinkled leather-yarn field is the identifying surface and should not be smoothed into ordinary knit.
