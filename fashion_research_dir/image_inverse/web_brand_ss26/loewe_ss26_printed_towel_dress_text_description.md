# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_printed_towel_dress

## text_description

A worn dress made from a 3-D printed fabric given the pile of velvet, described as a towel dress. The body is covered in a soft piled surface that reads like toweling or velvet rather than a flat weave, and the cloth itself is a printed construction. The dress is worn as one garment, with that piled, towel-like field as its identity from the upper body through the skirt. The pile has to stay visible; it is not a smooth satin column.
