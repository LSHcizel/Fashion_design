# The Row Spring/Summer 2026 therow_ss26_cashmere_robe_coat

## text_description

A worn cashmere coat with the ease of a robe. The coat is cashmere, soft in the hand, and cut with a robe-like looseness rather than a sharp shoulder or a belted trench waist. It is worn as an outer layer that hangs with that dressing-gown ease, the cashmere surface continuous from shoulder to hem. The identity is the robe-easy cashmere coat, not a structured overcoat and not the yellow cape.
