# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_fringe_coat

## text_description

A worn coat enlarged into a soft sculpture of twisting fringe layers. The coat is huge and glamorous, its body built from layers of fringe that twist around the silhouette rather than a flat tailored cloth. It is worn over the body as an outer wrap, with the twisting fringe making both the volume and the surface. The identity is that fringed, twisting coat-sculpture; the fringe layers have to stay readable as the coat itself.
