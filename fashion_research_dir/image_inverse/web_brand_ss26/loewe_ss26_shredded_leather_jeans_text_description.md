# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_shredded_leather_jeans

## text_description

A worn runway look that takes the jeans archetype and remakes it in shredded leather. The leg keeps a jeans-like cut, but the surface is leather broken into shreds rather than denim twill. The shredding is the identifying field: strips and opened edges run along the leg, so the garment reads as sportswear passed through leather craft. It is worn on the body as the lower half of the look, with the shredded leather leg as the point that must stay recognizable.
