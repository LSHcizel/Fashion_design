# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_scarf_dress

## text_description

A worn runway dress built from multiple draped scarf layers. The seams are left with their allowances turned outward, so each join becomes a cascade of ruffles running through the drape. The body is covered by overlapping scarf panels rather than a single cut of cloth, and the ruffled seam edges mark the path of the layers from the shoulder area down the length of the dress. The result is a fluid, layered garment whose identity is the exposed seam-allowance ruffle, not a plain slip dress.
