# Loewe Spring/Summer 2026 loewe_ss26_pleated_leather_shirt

## text_description

A worn button-down shirt made of leather that has been hand-pleated and spray-painted. The shirt keeps a button-down front, but the leather is broken into fine hand pleats and the surface carries spray-painted color over the pleats. The identity is that crafted leather shirting: pleat ridges catching paint, worn as an upper garment in place of a cloth button-down. The pleated, painted leather field has to remain visible across the front and sleeves.
