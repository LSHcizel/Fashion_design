# Alaïa Spring/Summer 2026 alaia_ss26_angled_knit_dress

## text_description

A worn fit-and-flare knit dress in the house's close-bodied knit, with the skirt cut on an angle instead of finished as a flounce. The bodice stays fitted and the skirt flares, but the hem and flare are angled rather than a rounded ruffle. It is worn as one knit garment on the body. The identifying change is that angled skirt: the flare has a slanted edge, not a flounced Alaïa hem.
