# The Row Spring/Summer 2026 therow_ss26_washed_jersey_layers

## text_description

A worn look in which white jersey underpinnings, slubbed like cloth that has been washed many times, are used as the visible outfit rather than hidden underwear. Inside and outside clothes are worn as one layered look, finished with heels. The jersey is white and slightly irregular from the slub, and more than one layer is readable on the body. The identity is that washed white jersey stack, dressed up by the heels, not a formal gown placed over invisible underwear.
