Theme: striped column. Concept: red and green vertical stripes on beige should stay a quiet knee-length set, with a sleeveless high-neck top, a red-edged gold-button placket, and a gathered skirt to the knee. Over that set the silhouette changes concept: a robe à la française with wide side panniers, a powdered coiffure, and a black beauty patch at the cheek, finished with heeled brocade mules. The quiet striped column and the rococo court dress do not share a theme.
