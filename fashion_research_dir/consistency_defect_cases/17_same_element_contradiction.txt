Theme: suspended ribbons. Concept: red, black, white, and pale beige ribbon strips fall from gold-tone shoulder accents and make the linear identity of the dress. The neckline is square to scoop between narrow black straps, and the same neckline is a high stand collar with no straps. The ground is vivid red with black vertical panels, and the same dress is entirely black with no red. The skirt is a loose midi column to mid-calf, and the same hem is a cropped top ending at the waist.
