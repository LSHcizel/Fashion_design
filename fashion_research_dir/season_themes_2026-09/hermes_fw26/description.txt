Heritage & provenance:
- Nadège Vanhée has led Hermès womenswear since 2014. Leather and equestrian codes are the house language.
- The collection title is Entre chien et loup, the French name for dusk, when a dog cannot be told from a wolf.
- Show notes invoke Hecate. The clothes stay muscular rather than mythological.

Show / location context:
- Fall/Winter 2026, Paris, built as twilight. Guests met the set by scent before they saw the clothes.
- A later Los Angeles chapter of the same season shifts from outdoor and equestrian exploration toward dance: a dancer leaving rehearsal.

Creative director intent:
- Twilight is a state of mind, not a black palette. Romance and utility are not opposites.
- Zippers and openings let the wearer reconfigure a garment through the day.
- In Los Angeles, the Carré becomes a dress: draped, then constructed. Ballet is grace of gesture and skill, crossed with the craftsman's skill.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Narrow, aerodynamic lines. Fluid overcoats with enormous Tuscan sheepskin collars. Zip-front mini dresses in inky blue that open onto a contrasting shirt. An orange ostrich-leather jumpsuit, belted.
- Jodhpurs, flat-heeled riding boots, glossy lambskin cycling shorts with aviator jackets, long riding coats, leather trenches, double-breasted blazers, cigarette trousers.
- Quilted silk printed with a cloud-strewn sky.
- Los Angeles: Carré dresses, wrap dresses, bustiers drawn from ballet slippers, leather parkas thrown over a performance body, a leather look touched by the American West.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Leather as muscle: ostrich engineered into second-skin jumpsuits and long dresses. Sheepskin collars. Quilted silk as the soft exception. Zips run diagonally or the full length of a dress.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Not simply black. Sulfur yellow, oxblood, forest green, iridescent burgundy, deep blue, moss, charcoal. Surfaces change in the light. A cloud-and-tower print from an Art Deco perspective study.

Accessories & finishing:
- The zip is the finishing. Pockets and openings are functional and visible. Do not add a charm story that leaves the leather.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- Agency: the same coat can open into another hour. Elegance is calibration. The Los Angeles fantasy is a dancer who has just put a jacket on and left the studio.
