Heritage & provenance:
- Nicolas Ghesquière at Louis Vuitton. The house's subject is travel, the monogram, and the trunk.
- He calls nature the greatest designer, and folklore an attempt to explain the forces of nature.

Show / location context:
- Fall/Winter 2026, titled Super Nature. The Cour Carrée of the Louvre was turned into an abstract mountain landscape by Jeremy Hindle, production designer of Severance: verdant prisms and pyramidal peaks, a pastoral scene inside a science-fiction dish.

Creative director intent:
- An anthropology of fashion: what mountain people from the Alps, Central Asia, and the Andes share. Endurance, protection, freedom of movement.
- Architectural clothing that can express different cultures and still bring them together.
- Show a known landscape as if it had never been seen. Wearable pieces stay inside the big scheme.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Shaggy capes, exaggerated and monumental shoulders, fur epaulettes that swallow the arm, cone-shaped hats, shearling caps, walking sticks draped with handbags.
- Cropped leather bombers, rounded cocoon coats, and smaller rounded coats that can actually be worn. Headgear reaches upward.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Fuzzy capes, cowbells, muddy-looking felt, shearling.
- Buttons like minerals. Heels like antlers. Vegetal furs. Leather grained and grooved to mirror wood. Invented leather flowers that decorate and protect.
- Animalier woven into canvas and denim. Craft melded with technology.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Moss, earth, and the green of the set. Pattern is animal and mineral, not a resort stripe. Flora and fauna leave an imprint rather than a cute print.

Accessories & finishing:
- Cowbells, walking sticks, shearling caps.
- Jewels reinterpret Man Ray. Parures studded with the nail-heads of a Louis Vuitton trunk.
- The Noé and other bags shift through time and invite a crossing.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- The wearer is equipped for weather and for moving on. Protection becomes fantastic without ceasing to be clothing. Nomadism is the house, not a backdrop.
