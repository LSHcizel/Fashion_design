Heritage & provenance:
- Jonathan Anderson at Christian Dior. He treats the giant past as the necessary start.
- Codes in play: the Bar jacket, the New Look waist and hip, Monsieur Dior's gray, Marc Bohan's monogram, Maria Grazia Chiuri's Book Tote, the framed Dior logo returned toward its original form.

Show / location context:
- Fall/Winter 2026 in the Jardin des Tuileries, spectators behind greenhouse glass. A walk in the park becomes a performance.
- Florals are asked to melt the set and the cloth together, as if water were reflecting off a printed silk.

Creative director intent:
- Redefine Dior's visual language with his own signatures while preserving the codes.
- Hold the real and the unreal in the same hand: garden tactility, then a Paris night and an office blazer.
- Levity matters. The heritage should not become solemn.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Opening: layered tutu skirts with frilled trains, under a textured gray peplum jacket, an ecru organza jacket with floral appliqué and tiny buttons, and a silk feather-printed brocade jacket with a mandarin collar.
- Bar jacket recut with a mandarin collar and rows of miniature buttons, or cropped and double-breasted over masses of hem ruffles.
- Cinched waists. Hips exaggerated with bolts of lace or loose pleats of wool and silk.
- Belle Époque ruffles turned into mini dresses with bouncing trains. Bourgeois tweed, gold-button blazers, shearling with a wave-like hem.
- Heavy evening coats worn slouchy. A blazer with a worn-in jean. Lace panels in eveningwear.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Organza, feather-printed brocade, lace, wool, silk, tweed, shearling, crystallized denim. Printed silk that looks wet.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Emblematic gray. Forest green on a scaled Book Tote. Polka dots. Water-lily and garden florals. Ecru.

Accessories & finishing:
- Water-lily shoes and jewelry. Lily-pad footwear. A green suede turtle bag with button eyes. Marc Bohan monogram as a simple crossbody. Book Tote scaled down and reprinted with the framed logo.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- A Parisian woman who can be in the garden, then at night, without dropping the house line. Play sits on a correct Dior shoulder.
