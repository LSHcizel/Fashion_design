Heritage & provenance:
- Maria Grazia Chiuri's second ready-to-wear show for Fendi.
- The line she has been using at the house: Less I. More Us.
- Clothing described by the house as a second skin, porous to emotion.

Show / location context:
- Spring/Summer 2027, Milan, in a hall of mirrors. The audience is separated from the runway and still watching.
- Invitations carry an e. e. cummings line: when I like my body, it is with your body.
- Soundtrack spirit from Raffaella Carrà, with Rumore and Adriano Celentano. Italian sensuality that is theatrical and knowingly camp.

Creative director intent:
- Start one layer closer to the skin. Bras, slips, lace rompers, and bloomers are the architecture worn before an outfit decides what it is.
- Sensuality does not have to mean exposure. The wearer sets the terms.
- A body can be strong and fragile, masculine and feminine, sometimes at once.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Plunge-front sheer midi. A going-out dress whose straps come undone. Salmon-pink lace-trimmed bloomers with a matching bralette. Black polished suiting.
- A slender Napoleonic cape coat over slouchy distressed denim. Plush opera coats.
- Headscarves. Layers stay light on the foot.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Lace, sheer silk, bead, sequin, fringe. The Baguette appears covered in sequins, fringe, and pattern.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Salmon pink against black suiting. Embellishment glints. It should not become a nightclub costume.

Accessories & finishing:
- The Baguette carries the theater. Headscarves frame the face. Straps that can be loosened are part of the garment's control, not damage.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- Getting dressed begins with the body, and the body has the final say. Glamour never costs ease. The shared wardrobe is intimate rather than a slogan.
