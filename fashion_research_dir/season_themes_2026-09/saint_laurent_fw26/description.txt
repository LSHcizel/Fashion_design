Heritage & provenance:
- Yves Saint Laurent's Le Smoking, remembered through Helmut Newton's 1975 Paris Vogue photograph on Rue Aubriot.
- Anthony Vaccarello treats repetition of the house signatures as the definition.

Show / location context:
- Fall/Winter 2026, Paris, at night, inside a simulacrum of a sleek modernist home.
- At the center, an enlarged bust that lived in Yves Saint Laurent's own apartment.
- The woman has a heavy smoked eye and slick, side-parted hair.

Creative director intent:
- Open with eight black trouser suits and close on another. The power suit is an insouciant shrug, not a swagger.
- The protagonist is Romy Schneider in Max et les Ferrailleurs, with the troubled heroines of Gore Vidal and Tennessee Williams beside her.
- Elegance tinged with ennui. The beauty of intimacy and vulnerability.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Black trouser suits with a sloped shoulder, a waist that narrows without constriction, and a plunging neckline.
- Vaccarello's own riff on the tuxedo.
- Sheer lace slips and dresses. Enormous fur coats as the louder counterweight.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Lace coated in silicone, so the slip gains solidity and a curious sheen. It is still transparent and no longer fragile nightwear.
- Fur for volume and confidence against the strict suit.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Black is the code. The silicone adds sheen rather than a second color story. Do not rescue the collection with a bright palette.

Accessories & finishing:
- The bust and the apartment are the scene, not garments to copy onto the body.
- Hair and the smoked eye are the styling that locks the Newton memory. Keep jewelry quieter than the suit and the coated lace.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- A woman who is looked at and slightly tired of it. Glamour includes vulnerability. The suit is both armor and a shrug.
