**Theme:**
Prada Spring/Summer 2027 is called The Skirt. Miuccia Prada and Raf Simons fix one familiar garment as a frame, like a rectangle or a painting, and ask how many ideas that single object can still hold.

**Inspiration:**
Shown on 22 September 2026 at Fondazione Prada, every look carries a skirt. Mrs Prada calls it the most flexible garment and a raw material for concepts: theoretical, conceptual, a frame, and finally a contradiction. Raf Simons treats it as Prada's version of the T-shirt, a simple shape that can carry a sexy mood, a luxurious mood, a working mood, or an at-ease mood. The combinations are deliberately illogical: a corporate shirt with a pleated skirt large enough to be held in the hand, a satin bra top with a clean Velcro skirt, a floral skirt slit to the hip under a nylon raincoat.

**Key aspects of the theme:**
**One parameter, many readings:**
The graphic skirt stays. Material, fastening, distance from the body, and the garment above it change. Newness comes from restudying a known object, not from inventing an unfamiliar category.

**Function left visible:**
Contrasting Velcro is both a closure and a ground for crystal embroidery, and it can be opened to change the shape. Enlarged metal snaps become jeweled. Crinoline leaves the inside of the skirt and becomes a visible shell or cage.

**Materials that misbehave:**
Re-Nylon and ciré enter pleats. Treated cotton resembles leather or suede. Crystal rebuilds lace. A chandelier skirt can be almost without cloth. Industrial and decorative techniques share the same silhouette.

**Styling as the second experiment:**
A sweater, a shirt, or a bra top rewrites the same skirt. The collection is an object analysis of one garment, and also a study of how the piece next to it changes the meaning.

**Sub-themes for chapter development:**
**1. The Skirt as a Frame**
One graphic skirt stays recognizable while length, pleat, bow, and slit change what the rectangle contains. Some versions are large enough to be held in the hand like a ballgown. The risk is making a primary shape feel new without inventing another garment category.

**2. Fastenings That Decorate**
Contrasting Velcro is both the closure and the ground for crystal embroidery, and opening it changes the skirt's shape. Enlarged metal snaps read as jewels when overlapping pleats move. Crinoline leaves the inside and becomes a visible shell or a cage around a messed pleat.

**3. A Familiar Cloth, Misread**
Re-Nylon and ciré enter the pleats. Treated cotton resembles leather or suede. Crystal rebuilds lace, and one skirt can be almost pure chandelier crystal over drab menswear wool. The silhouette stays honest while the surface lies about what it is.

**4. Illogical Pairs**
The same skirt family is worn with a corporate shirt, a satin bra top, and a nylon raincoat over a floral hip slit. Each pair is a different hour: work, seduction, weather. The contradiction is the collection, and color alone is not the update.
