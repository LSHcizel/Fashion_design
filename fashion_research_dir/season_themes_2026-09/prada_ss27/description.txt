Heritage & provenance:
- Miuccia Prada has long treated the skirt as her equivalent of the T-shirt: simple, primary, able to carry many ideas.
- She calls it a rectangle, a frame, a painting, and a contradiction.
- Crystal skirts recall the house's jeweled skirts of 2008 and 2010.

Show / location context:
- Spring/Summer 2027 womenswear, 22 September 2026, Fondazione Prada. The set reuses the men's lightbox floor.
- Every look includes a skirt. The lineup is a study of about sixty variations on one garment. The house title is The Skirt.

Creative director intent:
- Use the skirt as a raw material and a framework, not as a trend item.
- Narrow the parameter so material, function, embellishment, distance from the body, and the piece worn above it become visible.
- Raf Simons: the same skirt can be sexy, luxurious, for work, or at ease. The combinations are illogical on purpose.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- One graphic, linear skirt silhouette, from mini to midi, pencil to pleat. Some skirts are large enough to be held in the hand like a ballgown. One floral skirt is slit to the hip.
- Above the skirt: a corporate shirt, a satin bra top, a sweater, a nylon raincoat.
- Visible crinoline can cage a pleated skirt or sit outside it. Overlapping pleats can expose enlarged snaps.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Contrasting Velcro is both fastening and a ground for crystal embroidery, and it can be opened to change the shape.
- Enlarged metal snaps become jeweled.
- Crinoline leaves the inside and becomes a shell.
- Re-Nylon and ciré are pleated. Treated cotton resembles leather or suede. Crystal rebuilds lace. One skirt can be almost pure chandelier crystal. Drab menswear wool and pale peach crepe de chine with a scalloped hem both appear.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Neon fastening panels, vivid floral prints, Sixties "stupid little flowers," and drab wool. Color is one of the contradictions, not a separate story.

Accessories & finishing:
- The jewels are the snaps and the crystal on the closure. Do not add a separate jewelry story that leaves the skirt.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- Freedom is being able to wear one known garment in incompatible ways. The wearer questions whether newness requires a shape nobody has seen.
