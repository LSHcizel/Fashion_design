A straight mid-length skirt is the fixed frame, its identity made by a visible wrap-point closure at one hip and a clean rectangular front that stays legible even as the surface shifts in dense charcoal wool. Oversized matte snaps march diagonally from the hip toward the hem, turning the fastening into the skirt’s main graphic event and breaking the plain field with an engineered, almost office-coded interruption. Above it, a precision white poplin shirt sits buttoned high and severely tailored, but the hem is left open and slightly bloused so the skirt remains the governing line rather than a hidden base. The shirt sleeves are long and crisp, with narrow cuffs and a sharp collar that push the look into public discipline, while the skirt’s side opening adds a controlled swing without losing its frame. A slim black leather belt is absent; instead the fastening itself does the work. Black pointed pumps keep the line taut, and small silver stud earrings give only a cold highlight. A long set-in sleeve covers one arm to the wrist. The other side is sleeveless.
