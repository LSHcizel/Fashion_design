One photograph of one woman at one moment, from one camera. Show only what that camera sees on her body. No collage, no flat lay, no product shot, no extra views, no text.

An open leather shirt-jacket whose identity is the visible control layer beneath it: the outer shell is worn loose and slightly parted so a narrow zip-front riding vest becomes the real architecture of the look, framed by the jacket’s open front and high collar. The shirt-jacket is cut in dark moss leather with a blunt, workmanlike shoulder and a straight hem, its front edges held clean and spare rather than decorative. Underneath, the fitted vest in blackened olive or graphite sits close to the torso with a centered working zip and a disciplined, body-reading fit that remains readable through the opening. Slim riding trousers continue the line in matte dark wool or leather, narrow through thigh and knee, with a controlled break over tall knee boots in polished black or deep brown leather. The overall impression is one garment system that reveals its mechanism by staying open, where leather pressure and the zip’s incision make the body look trained, exact, and slightly nocturnal. The same jacket is double-breasted with two columns of buttons.
