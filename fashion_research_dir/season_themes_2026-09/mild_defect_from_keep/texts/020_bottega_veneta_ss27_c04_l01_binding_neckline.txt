A long city shirtcoat whose identity is an allover raffia-like woven skin, the whole surface reading as disciplined basket craft from shoulder to hem so the garment stays recognizable even if the material changes. The coat is cut straight and lightly boxy with a sharp shirt collar, long sleeves, and a clean front opening, while a narrow fringe path traces the lapel edge, continues down the opening, and flickers at the hem to draw the silhouette in one continuous line. It is worn open over a visible woven inner in the same earth-made language, a sleeveless longline gilet or basket-knit top in oat and bark tones that sits fully present in the opening and becomes the center of the look. Beneath, a lean straight trouser in dark clay or black keeps the body columnar and urban, and the whole palette stays raffia blond, tobacco, and muted brown with a small dusty lilac interruption worked into the weave. Slim leather sandals keep the finish grounded and wearable, with no resort softness. The same neckline drops in a deep V to the waist.
