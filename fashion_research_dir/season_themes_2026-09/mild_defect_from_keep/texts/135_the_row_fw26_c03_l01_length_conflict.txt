A single monolithic coat plane defines the look: a floor-skimming black coat in matte wool reads as one uninterrupted architectural mass from the broad shoulder to the hem, with almost no interruption beyond a clean front line and hidden fastening. The coat is cut oversized but exact, its shoulders extended and controlled, its body deep and severe, so the whole silhouette feels like a private façade rather than loose outerwear. Beneath it, wide-leg tailored trousers in the same dark register drop in a long, disciplined column, only the lower sweep visible at the opening and extending the vertical weight of the coat without breaking its calm. A close, minimal inner layer sits discreetly at the neck in black, never competing with the outer plane. The cloth surface stays matte and absorbing, with no sheen, no logo hardware, and no decorative noise; the power comes from proportion, length, and the continuous block of dark cloth. Low black shoes remain quiet under the hem, nearly disappearing into the line. The jacket is hip-length and cropped to the waist.
