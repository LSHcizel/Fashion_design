A strict long coat-dress worn open so a luminous same-theme inner becomes the look’s core: the opening frames a glowing flesh-pink mesh body and narrow skirt, making the radiant underlayer read as a second altar inside the shell. The outer coat falls in a disciplined vertical line to the ankle in smoked pearl-grey organza or lightweight crepe, with a single liturgical edge trace in acid mint running from the collar down the front opening and around the hem like a neon sermon line. Beneath it, the inner layer sits close to the body in sheer rose haze with bruised gold undertones, slightly translucent over the torso and hips so the silhouette feels lit from within rather than exposed. The coat’s shoulders are exact and severe, the front left unfastened, and the open panels create one clean frame for the glowing interior. Fine silver hardware is restrained to a small clasp at the throat and a slim metallic cuff at one wrist, while narrow black sandals or pointed heels keep the line lean and upright. The whole look is chapel-quiet, then electrically stained at the edges. The same jacket is plain black wool with no embroidery.
