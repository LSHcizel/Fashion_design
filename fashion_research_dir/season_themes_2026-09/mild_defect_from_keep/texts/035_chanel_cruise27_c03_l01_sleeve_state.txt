A boxy worker jacket becomes the look’s identity through an allover luminous pocket-map surface: tonal pocket blocks, seam-panel geometry, and chalk-white grid markings spread across the whole body of the jacket, so the construction itself reads as the decoration. The jacket sits slightly cropped and straight through the torso with a crisp stand collar, blunt front closure, and squared shoulders, in faded navy-black canvas or softened drill that still looks salt-bright and disciplined. Beneath it, a narrow black-and-white striped inner peeks at the opening, kept close to the body so the jacket remains the frame and not a costume change. The lower half is practical and upright, with engineered high-waist trousers in the same dark blue family, cut wide enough for movement but held clean and flat through the front. Small pocket flaps, tab closures, and topstitched seams stay visible as subordinate workwear detail. Low black leather shoes keep the line severe and wearable, while a compact chain strap bag or small structured accessory may sit close to the body, all in the same restrained coastal register. A long set-in sleeve covers one arm to the wrist. The other side is sleeveless.
