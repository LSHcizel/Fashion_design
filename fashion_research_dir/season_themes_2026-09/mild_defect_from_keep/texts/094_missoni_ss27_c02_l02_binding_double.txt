The knit surface stays light and breathable, with a slightly open gauge that suggests resort ease without turning sheer all over, and the trousers skim the leg with a relaxed hem that keeps the outline mobile. Palette moves through chalk, black, and a saline turquoise accent at the zigzag edges, with the inner top carrying a warmer lagoon tone for a clean pool-to-pavement read.
