Winged opening from a high throat defines the look: a long severe coat in blackened navy wool hangs straight through the body but parts from a close buttoned collar at the Adam’s apple, the front panels held away so they read like controlled wings instead of a normal lapel fall. The coat is long and lean, with a faint off-center drift to the opening and a slightly eased waist that lets the front breathe without losing precision. Beneath it, a matching inner layer in chalky silk crepe is fully on the body and clearly visible in the opening, its slim neckline, narrow sleeves, and clean hem forming the visible core rather than a hidden lining; a small flash of deep blue sits near the throat as the only sharp interruption. Narrow tailored trousers continue the vertical line in dark slate, pressed close and exact. The coat surface stays matte and spare, the edges crisp, the movement concentrated in the open front and suspended hem. Dark minimal leather shoes keep the silhouette grounded, understated and strange only in their sharp, edited shape. The same jacket is double-breasted with two columns of buttons.
