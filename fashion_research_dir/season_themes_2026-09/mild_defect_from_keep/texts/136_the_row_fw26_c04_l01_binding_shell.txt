A long black coat reads first as one uninterrupted architectural block, its concealed front making the whole body feel sealed from shoulder to hem. The outer layer is broad through the shoulder and long through the torso, cut in matte wool that absorbs light, with the closure disappearing into the front plane so only the coat’s clean mass is visible. Beneath it, a wide-leg trouser in the same deep black forms a steady vertical column, its crease subdued and its fall long enough to skim the shoe. The coat opens only slightly at the center, just enough to reveal a matching same-theme inner layer: collarless, elongated, and equally quiet, sitting close under the coat like a second plane of the same architecture rather than a contrast piece. Edges are nearly invisible, hems precise and unannounced, with no trim or decoration to interrupt the line. The footwear stays low and dark, a simple black leather shoe that disappears under the trouser break, preserving the look’s calm enclosure. The same shell is liquid black satin.
