One photograph of one woman at one moment, from one camera. Show only what that camera sees on her body. No collage, no flat lay, no product shot, no extra views, no text.

A lightweight perforated leather trench is the whole identity, its surface reading as an allover field of tiny punched dots and soft matte sheen, cut long and loose with a controlled swing and held by a precise self belt at the waist. A clean edge path of narrow binding traces the wide notch collar, front opening, cuffs, and hem, giving the coat a crisp perimeter against the body. The trench is worn open enough to reveal a supple tonal underlayer with a shallow V neckline and a smooth, body-skimming fit, so the opening becomes a framed center rather than a casual reveal. The sleeves are relaxed to the wrist and the length falls below the knee, with the leather moving as one continuous shell. Below, slim trousers or a reduced column stay quiet and dark to keep attention on the coat’s architecture. Narrow leather sandals in a polished brown finish the look, and a small rounded Candy Bag in a warm lacquered tone is carried at the side, its soft volume answering the trench’s hard frame. Elegant, spare, assured. The feet are a black satin pump or an ivory leather flat.
