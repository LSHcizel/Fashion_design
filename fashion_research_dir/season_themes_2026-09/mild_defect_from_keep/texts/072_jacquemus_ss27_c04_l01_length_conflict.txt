A bare-shoulder sun cutaway defines this look: a long column dress with one shoulder carved away into a clean, sun-bent opening, so the neckline turns asymmetrical and the exposed upper arm reads as part of the garment’s architecture. One side slit rises high enough to register stride without breaking the long vertical read, and the hem stays crisp and lightly bound so the silhouette feels drawn rather than floaty.
