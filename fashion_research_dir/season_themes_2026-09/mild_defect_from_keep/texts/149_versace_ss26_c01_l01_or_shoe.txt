One photograph of one woman at one moment, from one camera. Show only what that camera sees on her body. No collage, no flat lay, no product shot, no extra views, no text.

A body-hugging rib tee with a visibly shifted side seam and stretched neckline is the dominant idea, the fabric pulled long so it reads slightly skewed and lived-in rather than neat. The tee is white gone faintly opaque, with broken hand-painted face fragments and rubbed stripes skimming the chest and one sleeve, the print looking worn through heat and movement. Over it sits a sharp black satin vest worn open, its front edge traced by intermittent bright gold closures that stop and restart down the opening, making the edge path itself the event. The vest falls close to the torso with a narrow V, exposing the tee beneath and a hint of low coverage at the center, while the hem sits just above the waistband of washed blue jeans. The jeans are low on the hips, faded and slightly collapsed through the rise, with a soft worn knee and a narrow belt barely seated. On the feet, slim black heels keep the line clean and street-sharp. A single gold earring flashes near the jaw. The feet are a black satin pump or an ivory leather flat.
