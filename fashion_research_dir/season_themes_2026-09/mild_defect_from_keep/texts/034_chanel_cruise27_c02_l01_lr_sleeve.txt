A black dress whose identity is a large off-center bow locked into the back and reading like the garment’s hinge, with one precise knot mass and a long controlled tail that falls down the spine. The dress is a lean sleeveless column in deep ink black satin-faced crepe, cut close through the torso and held severe at the front, while the bow releases the rear into a moving vertical line that slightly shifts the silhouette. The neckline stays clean and high, the armholes crisp, and the lower half stays narrow before opening into a restrained back fall that suggests air without becoming a train. A faint pearl-black lining flashes only at the bow’s fold. Over it, nothing else interrupts the black discipline except a small, sharp evening earring near one ear. On the feet, slim black heels keep the line upright and formal, understated and exact. The left sleeve is a long wool sleeve in the cloth. The right sleeve is a long silk sleeve in plain ivory.
