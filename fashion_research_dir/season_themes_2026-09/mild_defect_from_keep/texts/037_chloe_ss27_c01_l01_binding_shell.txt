The dress is cut close through the torso, then loosens into a long, light fall, in chalk white cotton with tiny floral threadwork tucked between the eyelets so the surface carries a faint garden memory. A scalloped edge path traces the neckline, front opening, sleeve ends, and hem as one continuous border line, making the perimeter feel drawn by hand. The bodice sits high and neat, the short sleeves are gently set, and the skirt descends in a soft vertical line with sheer density shifting at the lower half where the cutwork opens more visibly to shadow and skin tone beneath. The look stays within one garment identity, intimate and exact, with pale ivory underlayers only flickering through the openings rather than forming a separate statement. Slim ivory sandals stay understated and close to the foot, keeping the eye on the perforated surface and its fragile, self-aware edge. The same shell is liquid black satin.
