A long fishermen coat defined by one irregular storm-path edge tracing the front opening, collar, and hem in embroidered binding, so the silhouette reads like a shoreline drawn in thread. The coat is a fog-white to washed-slate shell with a broad, slightly swung body, matte weathered surface, and a clean open front that keeps the edge path visible from throat to lower hem. It is worn open over a broderie anglaise inner look that stays legible in the opening: a pale cotton dress with floral cutwork, soft eyelet rows, and a matching embroidered blouse effect at the upper body, the delicate surface framed rather than hidden by the shell. The coat’s sleeves fall long and easy with a protective width, while the inner hem and sleeve openings of the broderie layer show beneath the shell as the only visible tenderness. Slim dark tights or bare legs keep the line spare, and simple low leather shoes ground the look without competing with the coat. The result feels weatherproof, intimate, and sharply Chloé.
