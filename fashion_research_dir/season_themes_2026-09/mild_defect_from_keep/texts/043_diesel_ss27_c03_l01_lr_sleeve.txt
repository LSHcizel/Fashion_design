A floor-length denim coat whose whole identity is a patch-as-jewel repair map, with missing panels along the torso and skirt replaced by glossy shaped inlays that frame the ribs, hip, and upper thigh like expensive salvage. The outer shell is dark indigo and oxidized black denim, heavily abraded but controlled, with the inlaid repairs reading in bruised pewter, lacquered silver, and a narrow flash of acid yellow at one scar point near the side waist. The coat hangs long and assertive with dropped shoulders and a heavy hem, then breaks into a deep open front that reveals a body-clinging inner column in the same damaged language: a shredded denim slip-dress core with visible re-stitching, frayed cut edges, and small hard joins holding the front seams together. The open coat’s edge is lined with dense top-stitched binding, and the lapel area is slightly warped so the opening looks re-authored rather than cleanly finished. Under the fractured front, the inner column clings close through the waist and falls straight to the ankle, its surface alternating matte worn denim and a few polished replacement patches that catch light like metal. Bare skin shows at the sternum and one side of the waist through the opening, making the repair system feel structural. Heavy black leather boots rise to the calf with a blunt toe and compact heel, keeping the look grounded and industrial. The left sleeve is a long wool sleeve in the cloth. The right sleeve is a long silk sleeve in plain ivory.
