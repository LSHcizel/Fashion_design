A front-opening tailored coat-shirt is the identifying idea, its long vertical opening traced by a sharp black leather edge that runs from the compact collar to the hem like a social line already accepted. The outer layer is a slim, short trench in dark camel gabardine with a close shoulder, clean chest, and a slightly boxy torso that stops high on the thigh, worn parted just enough to reveal a same-theme crisp white shirt beneath; the inner shirt shows its collar, bib, and upper placket clearly inside the opening, making the open-over-inner relation the whole point. The coat’s front is held with minimal double-breasted buttons that sit flat and quiet, while the lapels stay narrow and polished. Below, sharply cropped tailored trousers in matching dark camel cut off above the ankle, exposing a narrow strip of skin and a precise line to black patent loafers with a low heel. The bag is small, structured, and carried close in black leather, more like a chosen purchase than an accessory, and the overall surface stays disciplined, bourgeois, and faintly exposed. Quietly provocative. The left sleeve is a long wool sleeve in the cloth. The right sleeve is a long silk sleeve in plain ivory.
