A collar-close inner layer in the same muted register sits visible in the opening, its front softened by a lightly crinkled surface so the exposed center feels lived-in rather than decorative. The trousers stay slim and vertical, matching the coat in discipline and tone, with a clean line that preserves the house precision under the textured outer skin. Nothing is voluminous; the looseness is only in the surface and the edge, where pressure has polished the borders into a quiet bruise.
