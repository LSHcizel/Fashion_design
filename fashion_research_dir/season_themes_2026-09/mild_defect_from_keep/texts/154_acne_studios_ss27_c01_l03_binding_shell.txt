The blazer is cut long past the hip in matte black wool-gabardine, with a softened lapel break and a slightly extended front overlap that makes the body feel gently displaced rather than collapsed. The surface stays clean and dry, with only the tailoring grain and seam edges giving it structure. It is worn over a pale dusty-pink inner layer that shows just at the V of the opening and at the lower edge, like a quiet internal weather beneath the dark shell. Beneath, the narrow trousers are straight and lean in the same black tone, falling with a controlled break to keep the line vertical and severe. The look is finished with slim black leather shoes that stay minimal and close to the ground. Quietly off-kilter. The same shell is liquid black satin.
