**Theme:**
Bottega Veneta Spring/Summer 2027 lightens the house without leaving its craft. Louise Trotter calls the season an in-betweenness: between one season and the next, between a polished language and a more organic one, between identities that are still settling.

**Inspiration:**
This is Trotter's third Bottega Veneta show, and the first in which her handwriting is fully visible. She describes the house as a house of craft, and this season looks for techniques that keep the handwork while adding lightness and wearability. Intrecciato remains the logo, then expands from a leather weave into a metaphor for skills and people coming together. Venetian lace, crochet, straw, ribbon, and fringe supply the air. Clothes flutter, leather becomes featherlight, and a little imperfection is left visible as evidence of the hand.

**Key aspects of the theme:**
**Craft, worn lighter:**
Woven leather is no longer the whole sentence. Basket-weave cotton, crochet overshirts, macramé tanks, and an enlarged intrecciato tote that reads as raffia carry the same intelligence at a lower weight.

**Fringe as movement:**
Fringe hangs from hip scarves, turns into bubble skirts whose hems lift into ellipses, nearly sweeps the floor from a straw bag, and draws a trompe-l'oeil floral on a dress. The decoration is what makes the garment move.

**Polished beside organic:**
A knotted coat over a cotton dress, a fringed scarf-belt on a relaxed suit, and a tangerine leather midi sit with butter yellow and lilac. Tailoring loosens. The finish is not uniformly precious.

**Realism after the fantasy:**
The clothes are meant for life past the show. Lightness here is wearable, not transparent for its own sake. Lace is present, and it is not the only way the collection breathes.

**Sub-themes for chapter development:**
**1. Intrecciato, Abstracted**
The weave remains the logo, then leaves the classic lattice. It appears as a coat knotted at one side over a cotton dress, a basket-weave T-shirt, and an enlarged tote that reads as raffia. Craft is still the identity after the leather weight drops.

**2. Fringe That Changes the Hem**
Fringe is the movement, not a trim. Hip scarves, bubble skirts whose hems lift into ellipses, a trompe-l'oeil floral drawn in fringe, and a straw bag whose fringe nears the floor all change the silhouette while walking. Butter yellow, lilac, tangerine, and lavender are choices inside that motion.

**3. Venetian Handwork**
Crochet overshirts, lace, ribbon, straw, and a macramé tank over a gauzy pleated maxi keep a visible irregularity, the evidence of the hand. Leather stays in the look and becomes featherlight. Openwork is the lightness. Sheer fabric is not required to do that job.

**4. Between Polish and Ease**
A relaxed suit with a fringed scarf-belt sits beside a scoop-neck leather midi. Polished and organic are one passage, the way spring opens into summer. The chapter should feel suspended between two states, still wearable past the show.
