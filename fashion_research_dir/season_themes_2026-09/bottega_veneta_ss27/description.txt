Heritage & provenance:
- Intrecciato is the house mark, as distinctive as a monogram. Louise Trotter calls Bottega a house of craft, and says craft is the logo.
- This is her third collection, and the first in which her own handwriting is fully visible.

Show / location context:
- Spring/Summer 2027, Milan. The season is treated as a passage: spring opening into summer, layers beginning to fall away.

Creative director intent:
- Find techniques that keep extraordinary handwork while adding lightness and wearability.
- Name the condition in-betweenness: suspended between one state and another, between seasons and between identities.
- A language of polished things beside a language of more organic things. Leave evidence of the hand.

Silhouette & garment vocabulary:
- Clothes flutter and barely touch the body while still drawing its contour. Tailoring loosens.
- A longline coat knotted at one side over a light cotton dress. A fringed scarf-belt on a relaxed suit. A basket-weave T-shirt. A scoop-neck leather midi. A macramé tank with a gauzy, lightly pleated maxi skirt.
- Fringe bubble skirts whose hems lift into ellipses while walking. A dress on which fringe draws a trompe-l'oeil floral.

Materials, craft & surface:
- Lighter, crisper cloth; airy lace and crochet from Venetian knitting and stitching; straw, ribbon, leather that has become featherlight.
- An enlarged intrecciato tote that reads as raffia. Crochet overshirts. Fringe on a straw bag long enough to near the floor.

Color, stripe & graphic codes:
- Mostly neutral, with butter yellow, lilac, tangerine, a lavender fringe skirt, and one earthy mixed fringe skirt. Color is a choice inside the craft, not a print story.

Accessories & finishing:
- The hip scarf, the floor-reaching straw fringe, and the raffia-like tote are part of the garment's movement. They are not add-on charms.

Mood, liberation & wearer fantasy:
- Lightness without relying only on sheer fabric. Imperfection is the proof of making. The clothes are for a life past the show, still poetic.
