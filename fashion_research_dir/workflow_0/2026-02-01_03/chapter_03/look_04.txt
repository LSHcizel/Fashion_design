**Look 4: The Anatomical Ghost**

**1. The Outerwear: The Split-Axis Skeletal Frock Coat**
This centerpiece is a rigorous exercise in architectural duality, constructed as a long-line, double-breasted frock coat that is vertically bisected down the spine and center-front. The left hemisphere is crafted from a dense Midnight Blue wool gabardine, while the right is a stark Pitch Black matte silk crepe. This contrast in textile density creates a subtle structural tension, as the light absorbs differently into each side, visually "splitting" the model’s form.

    *   **The Shoulder:** The shoulders are razor-sharp and significantly exaggerated, featuring internal padding and a roped sleeve head that extends the natural line upward and outward, creating a monumental, statuesque presence inspired by 19th-century dandyism.

    *   **The Lapel:** A dramatic, oversized peak lapel dominates the chest, pointing toward the shoulders. The lapel is executed in tonal bonded velvet, providing a tactile, light-absorbing contrast to the wool and crepe body.

    *   **The Closure:** The fastening features a low-slung, double-breasted arrangement of six buttons. The buttons are custom-molded from Oxidized Silver resin, resembling antique mirror fragments trapped in liquid.

    *   **The Length & Cut:** The coat is cut to a mid-calf length with a snatched, corseted waist achieved through internal boning, creating a severe hourglass silhouette that flares slightly at the hem.

    *   **The Secret:** The back of the coat features an intricate "Mirrored Silver Bullion Skeletal Embroidery." Hand-stitched silver threads and tiny shards of mirrored glass trace the anatomical structure of the human spine and ribcage, acting as a literal reflection of the wearer's internal structure that only reveals itself as the model turns.

**2. The Foundation: The Bettina Ghost & Smoking Trouser**
The base of the look explores the tension between the hidden and the revealed, utilizing sheer layers against rigid tailoring.

    *   **The Shirt/Top:** A "Bettina" blouse rendered in sheer Ink Black organza. It features a high Victorian collar and a hidden placket, allowing the model’s skin to appear as a spectral layer beneath the heavy frock coat.

    *   **The Trouser/Bottom:** High-waisted "smoking" style trousers in Midnight Blue heavy silk moiré. The "watered" pattern of the moiré creates a shifting, hallucinatory surface that mimics a dark lake in motion.

    *   **The Fit & Line:** The trousers feature a sharp, pressed crease and a slim-tapered leg, elongated by a side stripe of faceted Obsidian beads that shimmer like a broken mirror with every step.

**3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware**
The look is unified by metallic accents and rigid Victorian-inspired hardware.

    *   **The Cuffs:** The blouse features oversized, 6-inch stiffened velvet "Bettina" cuffs. These are a stark Ink Black, providing a heavy, architectural weight at the wrist that contrasts with the sheer organza sleeve.

    *   **The Waist:** The waist is defined by a built-in silk faille cummerbund in Pitch Black, which integrates seamlessly into the trouser waistband to further snatch the silhouette.

    *   **The Hands:** The model wears a single, structural "Doppelgänger" ring—a dual-band piece in Oxidized Silver that spans two fingers, holding a large, raw-cut smoky quartz.

    *   **Additional Details:** A high Victorian choker in Midnight Blue velvet cinches the neck, featuring a central "mirrored eye" charm—a small, hand-painted glass cabochon that symbolizes the gaze of the observer.

**4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance**
The look concludes with a sense of grounded, aristocratic authority.

    *   **The Shoe:** A pointed-toe "Stiletto Combat" boot in polished Ink Black box-calf leather. The heel is a thin, architectural spike in Oxidized Silver, and the sole features a subtle platform to increase the model's height and commanding presence.

    *   **The Stance:** The height of the heel and the snatch of the corset create a rigid, upright posture. The model moves with a slow, deliberate cadence, emphasizing the "divided" nature of the garment as the colors shift from blue to black.

    *   **The Palette:** A masterclass in "Noir Depths," the palette uses Midnight Blue and Ink Black as the primary anchors, with Oxidized Silver and mirrored glass providing cold, rhythmic flashes of light that evoke the "I Am Your Mirror" theme.
