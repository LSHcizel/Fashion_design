Theme (condensed):
This theme explores the symbiotic relationship between identity and reflection, focusing on the fluidity of gender and the blurring of traditional masculine and feminine boundaries within the House of Givenchy.

Key aspects (condensed):
- The Noir Romanticism of the Double: Drawing from Givenchy’s history of dark romanticism, the theme employs a palette dominated by stark blacks, optical whites, and midnight blues to emphasize contrast.
- Androgynous Tailoring and the Masculine-Feminine Mirror: The core of the theme lies in the seamless integration of traditional menswear elements into the feminine wardrobe.
- Reflective Materiality and Surface Tension: To visually represent the "mirror" concept, the collection utilizes high-sheen fabrics and reflective finishes.
- Intellectual Nomadism and Utilitarian Chic: Inspired by Annemarie Schwarzenbach’s travels, the theme incorporates functional, utilitarian details such as oversized pockets, trench-inspired capes, and sturdy leather accessories.
