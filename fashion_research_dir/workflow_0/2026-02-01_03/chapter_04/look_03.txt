**Look 3: The Archivist’s Reflection**

A centerpiece of functional luxury, this gilet is constructed from sun-bleached bonded leather in a deep, weathered Burnt Umber. The leather has a matte, dust-coated texture that suggests years of travel, yet it is bonded to a high-luster silk-satin interior in Desert Sand that catches the light with every movement, personifying the "Mirror" theme through hidden radiance.

    *   **The Shoulder:** The gilet features a sharp, extended shoulder line, achieving a dandy-inspired silhouette without the use of sleeves. The armholes are finished with a raw-edged leather binding, emphasizing the garment's rugged, utilitarian origin.

    *   **The Lapel:** The piece is designed with a clean, collarless V-neckline that allows the delicate textures of the foundation layers to remain visible. The edges are precision-cut and reinforced with tonal topstitching.

    *   **The Closure:** A hidden placket features a series of industrial-grade magnetic closures, providing a sleek, uninterrupted front. This minimalist fastening ensures the focus remains on the complex pocket architecture.

    *   **The Length & Cut:** The gilet is cut to a structured hip-length, with a slightly boxy fit that allows for effortless layering. Side slits allow the fabric to swing, revealing the high-shine interior.
