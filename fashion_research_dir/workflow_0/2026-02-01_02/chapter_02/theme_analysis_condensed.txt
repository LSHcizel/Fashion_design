Theme (condensed):
"I Am Your Mirror" is a conceptual exploration of identity, duality, and the psychological "mirror stage" where the self is recognized through the eyes of the other.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Reflective Materiality and Distortion: The collection utilizes textures that play with light and perception, such as high-gloss patent leathers, metallic silks, and mirrored embroidery.
- The Androgynous Silhouette: Central to the theme is the erasure of traditional gender boundaries, inspired by Annemarie Schwarzenbach’s personal style.
- Monochromatic Duality: The color palette leans heavily on the classic Givenchy black and white, representing the binary nature of the mirror.
- Intellectual Bohemianism: Drawing from Schwarzenbach's life as a traveler and thinker, this aspect introduces a sense of refined utility and "traveler’s grace" into the haute couture world.
