Theme (condensed):
I Am Your Mirror is a conceptual exploration of identity, duality, and the psychological interplay between the observer and the observed.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Radical Androgyny and the 'Garçonne' Silhouette: Central to the collection is the blurring of masculine and feminine codes, inspired by Schwarzenbach’s personal wardrobe.
- Reflective Materiality and Chiaroscuro: To embody the "Mirror" element, the collection employs a sophisticated play of light and shadow.
- The Nomadic Aristocrat: This aspect explores the tension between Givenchy’s heritage of refined elegance and the utilitarian needs of a wanderer.
- Symmetry and Fragmented Duality: The theme of the mirror is further explored through design techniques that play with symmetry and its disruption.
