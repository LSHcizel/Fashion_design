Theme (condensed):
"I Am Your Mirror" serves as a profound exploration of identity, duality, and the fluid boundaries between the self and the observer.

Key aspects (condensed):
- The Androgynous Silhouette: Central to the theme is the erasure of rigid gender lines, drawing directly from Schwarzenbach’s personal style.
- Optical Reflectivity and Luster: To embody the "Mirror" concept literally and figuratively, the collection utilizes a range of high-shine fabrics and reflective surfaces.
- The Monochrome Dialectic: The color story is dominated by a stark, sophisticated contrast between deep blacks and crisp whites, representing the binary of a reflection and its shadow.
- Utilitarian Intellectualism: Reflecting Schwarzenbach’s life as a daring journalist and traveler, the collection incorporates functional elements—such as oversized pockets, belted waists, and durable gabardines—into high-fashion contexts.
