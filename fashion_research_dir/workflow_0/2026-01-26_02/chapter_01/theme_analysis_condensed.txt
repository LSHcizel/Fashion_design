Theme (condensed):
"I Am Your Mirror" is a profound exploration of identity, duality, and the dissolution of gender boundaries within the house of Givenchy.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Androgynous Fluidity: This aspect focuses on the seamless blending of masculine and feminine silhouettes.
- The Mirroring of Eras: The theme acts as a bridge between the avant-garde spirit of the 1930s and modern-day minimalism.
- Sartorial Duality (Light vs. Structure): A critical design element is the juxtaposition of rigid fabrics with liquid textures.
- Reflective Materiality and Symmetry: To physically manifest the "mirror" theme, the collection employs silvered fabrics, metallic embroidery, and symmetrical patterns.
- Nomadic Intellectualism: Reflecting Annemarie Schwarzenbach’s life as a traveler, this aspect introduces a sense of refined utilitarianism.
