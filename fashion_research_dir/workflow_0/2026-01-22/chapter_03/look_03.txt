**Look textual description**

**Look 3: The Fractured Reflection**

**1. The Outerwear: Deconstructed 1930s Tuxedo Jacket**
A stark Obsidian Black jacket in matte bonded wool that serves as a rigid frame for the more delicate layers beneath. The garment is a study in controlled disruption, featuring a sharp, architectural silhouette that draws from 1930s masculine evening wear but is subverted by intentional structural breaks.

    *   **The Shoulder:** The shoulder is hyper-extended and padded to create a formidable, squared-off line. The sleeve heads are crisp, with the sleeves themselves featuring vertical slits along the inner seam to reveal flashes of the sheer Optic White Chantilly lace lining within.

    *   **The Lapel:** Elongated, peak lapels in tonal Obsidian Black silk satin create a deep "V" that draws the eye toward the shattered waistline. The satin reflects a dull, oily sheen, contrasting with the light-absorbing matte wool of the body.

    *   **The Closure:** A single, low-slung button in polished Obsidian horn fastens at the high hip, allowing the jacket to fall open and expose the intricate corsetry underneath. This low fastening point emphasizes a long, vertical line.

    *   **The Length & Cut:** The jacket is cropped sharply at the back while the front panels extend to the mid-thigh, creating a dramatic high-low rhythm. The cut is clinical and precise, ignoring the natural waist to prioritize a geometric, boxy form.

    *   **The Secret:** Along the spine, a line of "Cracked" Mirror Embroidery in Glacial Silver metallic thread and micro-beads creates a jagged, tactile texture that catches the light like a fissure in stone.

**2. The Foundation: Shattered Mylar Corsetry & Chantilly Lace**
The core of the look is a high-contrast layering of textures that embodies the "vulnerable fortress." An Optic White Translucent Chantilly 'Ascot' Blouse is worn beneath a structured, light-refracting bodice.

    *   **The Shirt/Top:** The blouse is crafted from gossamer-thin Chantilly lace in Optic White, featuring a high, stiffened collar and a voluminous integrated ascot tie that spills over the jacket's lapels. The lace pattern is ecclesiastical and delicate, creating a halo of white against the model’s throat.

    *   **The Trouser/Bottom:** A floor-length skirt made of laser-cut lambskin leather in Obsidian Black. The leather is perforated in intricate 'Noir' lace patterns, providing a heavy, structural weight that moves with a rhythmic, clanking swing. Beneath the leather, a Glacial Silver silk slip provides a metallic glow through the cutouts.

    *   **The Fit & Line:** The look is defined by the "Shell and the Ghost" silhouette: the rigid, sharp-shouldered jacket (the shell) creates a protective perimeter around the clinging lace and the metallic, fractured waist (the ghost).

**3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware**
The details focus on the interaction between the organic delicacy of lace and the industrial sharpness of the mirror elements.

    *   **The Cuffs:** The jacket sleeves end in traditional five-button surgeon’s cuffs, but the buttons are replaced by small, hand-stitched fragments of Glacial Silver Mylar, mirroring the bodice.

    *   **The Waist:** The focal point is a Shattered Mylar Mirror Corset in Glacial Silver. Constructed from hand-stitched, jagged fragments of acrylic and Mylar, it resembles a broken looking-glass. It is held in place by an internal technical harness of Obsidian Black grosgrain ribbon.

    *   **The Hands:** The model wears sheer, elbow-length tulle gloves in Optic White, finished with a single "cracked glass" ring in polished silver on the middle finger.

    *   **Additional Details:** The leather lace skirt features exposed industrial silver zippers at the hips, allowing the volume to be adjusted and revealing more of the silver silk underlayer.

**4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance**
The look is grounded by footwear that emphasizes the nomadic isolation and strength of the "Angelic Noir" character.

    *   **The Shoe:** A heavy, square-toed platform boot in polished Obsidian Black leather. The heel is a transparent acrylic block embedded with silver leaf, creating the illusion that the model is walking on shards of frozen light.

    *   **The Stance:** The weight of the leather skirt and the rigidity of the mirror corset dictate a stoic, vertical posture. The model moves with a measured, deliberate stride that causes the Mylar fragments to throw rhythmic, blinding flashes of Glacial Silver across the dark environment.

    *   **The Palette:** A high-contrast tri-tonal scheme. The Obsidian Black provides the void-like depth, the Optic White lace creates a spectral luminosity around the face, and the Glacial Silver mirror-work acts as the "fractured" medium that connects the two extremes.
