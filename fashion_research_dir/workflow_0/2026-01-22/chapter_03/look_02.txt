**Look 2: The Refracted Gentleman**

**1. The Outerwear: Deconstructed 1930s Tuxedo Jacket**
A stark reimagining of classic evening wear, this jacket is rendered in matte Obsidian Black wool crepe. It serves as a rigid, masculine frame that purposely contrasts with the fluid, shimmering layers beneath. The garment features a disruptive construction where the traditional structure is sliced and reassembled to reveal glimpses of the "internal" self.

*   **The Shoulder:** The shoulder line is exaggerated and razor-sharp, utilizing internal structured padding to create a broad, 1930s-inspired silhouette. The sleeve heads are slightly roped, but the sleeves themselves are slit from the elbow to the wrist, allowing the silver silk lining to spill out like liquid metal as the model moves.

*   **The Lapel:** Elongated, peak lapels in a tonal Obsidian Black silk-satin extend down to the mid-ribcage. The sharp verticality of the lapels draws the eye downward, emphasizing the height and lean stature of the "Angelic Noir" aesthetic.

*   **The Closure:** A single, low-slung fastening featuring a hand-carved Obsidian horn button. When fastened, the jacket creates a deep 'V' that frames the shattered mirror corset, while the hem is cropped slightly higher at the front to reveal the bias-cut slip.

*   **The Length & Cut:** The jacket is cut close to the torso but features a dramatic, floor-sweeping "tail" at the back, mimicking a formal morning coat but with the raw edges and deconstructed feel of modern couture.

*   **The Secret:** Along the center back seam, 'Cracked' Mirror Embroidery runs like a shimmering spine. Micro-beads and silver metallic thread are meticulously stitched to simulate the appearance of light refracting through a jagged fracture in glass, visible only as the model turns.

**2. The Foundation: The Silver Ghost & Shattered Corset**
The foundation is a study in "Spectral Fluidity," combining the softness of a second skin with the aggressive brilliance of reflective shards.

*   **The Shirt/Top:** In place of a shirt, the model wears a Crystalline Subversion corset constructed from hand-stitched fragments of Glacial Silver Mylar and polished acrylic. The fragments are arranged in a chaotic, non-repeating pattern that mimics a smashed mirror, catching the spotlight to create rhythmic, blinding flashes of light.

*   **The Trouser/Bottom:** Beneath the corset is a floor-length, liquid silk bias-cut slip in Glacial Silver. The heavyweight satin clings to the hips and thighs before flaring into a subtle puddle train. The cool, metallic tone of the silver mimics a mercury-like surface, flowing with organic ease against the rigid tuxedo jacket.

*   **The Fit & Line:** The look creates a "vertical fracture"—a sharp, dark outer shell containing a brilliant, light-reflective core. The juxtaposition of the structured wool and the fluid silk creates a silhouette that is simultaneously defensive and incredibly vulnerable.

**3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware**
The details reinforce the "vulnerable fortress" concept through a mix of high-shine metallics and deep, light-absorbing textures.

*   **The Cuffs:** The cuffs are non-functional, left open and unbuttoned to allow the slit sleeves to flare. The interior is lined in Optic White silk organza, providing a momentary flash of "purity" against the Obsidian Black exterior.

*   **The Waist:** The waist is sharply defined by the Shattered Mylar corset, which sits high on the ribcage, compressing the silhouette and creating an architectural transition between the jacket and the slip.

*   **The Hands:** The model wears a single, opera-length glove on the right hand made from laser-cut Obsidian Black lambskin leather in an ecclesiastical lace pattern. This "Noir Lace" adds a sense of dark romanticism and tactile complexity to the hand.

*   **Additional Details:** The jacket features "phantom" pockets—welted pocket details without actual openings—maintaining the clinical, minimalist lines of the Sartorial Fortification theme.

**4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance**
The footwear grounds the ethereal, light-catching elements of the upper body with a heavy, monolithic base.

*   **The Shoe:** A squared-toe platform boot in high-shine Obsidian Black patent leather. The sole is thick and industrial, providing a heavy, rhythmic "clack" against the runway surface that echoes the dissonant cello score.

*   **The Stance:** The height of the platform and the weight of the floor-sweeping silk create a deliberate, gliding stride. The model carries a posture of stoic isolation, with the heavy shoulders of the jacket creating a protective frame for the fragile, shimmering core.

*   **The Palette:** A high-contrast tri-tonal scheme. The Obsidian Black provides the "void" or shadow, the Glacial Silver represents the "fractured" reflection of the mirror, and the Optic White linings offer a hidden, clinical purity. The overall effect is one of stark chiaroscuro, where the model appears to be emerging from darkness into a splintered light.
