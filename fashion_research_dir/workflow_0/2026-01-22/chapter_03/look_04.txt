**Look textual description**

**Look 4: The Monolithic Fragment**

**1. The Outerwear: Deconstructed Hooded Tuxedo Jacket**
The centerpiece of this look is a deconstructed 1930s tuxedo jacket rendered in heavy, matte Obsidian Black wool. The garment is a study in "Sartorial Fortification," acting as a rigid frame for the delicate layers beneath. The most striking feature is the integration of a "Cavernous Chiaroscuro Hood" that rises seamlessly from the back panel, designed to cast a deep, triangular shadow over the face.

    *   **The Shoulder:** The shoulder line is extreme and architectural, featuring internal roping that creates a sharp, horizontal extension. This exaggerated width emphasizes the "fortress" silhouette, making the wearer appear both imposing and shielded.

    *   **The Lapel:** Elongated, floor-length peak lapels are faced in tonal Obsidian Black silk satin. They descend in a sharp "V" shape, drawing the eye downward to the waist and creating a sense of vertical dominance.

    *   **The Closure:** A single, low-slung button made of polished Glacial Silver acrylic secures the jacket at the narrowest part of the waist. This creates a dramatic hourglass tension against the rigid shoulders.

    *   **The Length & Cut:** The jacket is cropped sharply at the ribcage in the front, while the back tails extend into a long, dramatic sweep. The sleeves are slit from the elbow to the wrist, allowing the inner layers of lace to spill out during movement.

    *   **The Secret:** Along the spine, the jacket features "Cracked" Mirror Embroidery. Using micro-beads and metallic silver thread, the embroidery mimics a jagged fracture running down the vertebrae, symbolizing the "breaking point" of the private self.

**2. The Foundation: The Lace 'Ascot' & Leather 'Noir' Skirt**
The foundation juxtaposes "Spectral Fluidity" with "Dark Romanticism." A sheer Optic White Chantilly lace "Ascot" blouse provides a ghostly, angelic contrast to the heavy Obsidian Black outerwear.

    *   **The Shirt/Top:** The blouse is constructed from gossamer-thin Chantilly lace in Optic White. It features a high, stiffened Victorian collar with an integrated oversized ascot tie that is knotted loosely at the throat. The transparency of the lace reveals the Glacial Silver silk slip worn beneath, which catches the light like liquid metal.

    *   **The Trouser/Bottom:** The bottom is a floor-sweeping skirt crafted from Obsidian Black lambskin leather. The leather has been intricately laser-cut into a "Noir Lace" pattern, mirroring the ecclesiastical motifs of the blouse but in a heavy, structural medium. The weight of the leather ensures the skirt maintains a columnar, statuesque form.

    *   **The Fit & Line:** The look follows a strict vertical line, with the transparency of the upper torso (The Ghost) transitioning into the impenetrable, patterned weight of the leather skirt (The Shell).

**3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware**
The details emphasize the "Angelic Noir" aesthetic through sharp contrasts in texture and light-reflecting surfaces.

    *   **The Cuffs:** The jacket sleeves feature functional surgeon’s cuffs with four Glacial Silver buttons. When the sleeves are left unbuttoned, the sheer Optic White lace of the blouse cuffs is revealed, creating a layered, "slashed" effect.

    *   **The Waist:** A hidden technical harness in Obsidian Black grosgrain ribbon is worn over the lace blouse but under the jacket, cinching the waist and ensuring the heavy leather skirt is suspended with clinical precision.

    *   **The Hands:** The model wears sheer, fingerless Chantilly lace gloves in Optic White, extending to the mid-forearm. A single "Shattered Mirror" ring in Glacial Silver acrylic is worn on the index finger, providing a sharp, crystalline focal point.

    *   **Additional Details:** The leather lace of the skirt is backed with Glacial Silver silk organza in specific panels, causing the "shattered" light to peek through the intricate laser-cut perforations as the model walks.

**4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance**
The look is grounded by footwear that reinforces the "vulnerable fortress" concept, providing a sense of nomadic isolation and strength.

    *   **The Shoe:** A heavy, square-toed platform boot in polished Obsidian Black box-calf leather. The heel is a hollow, architectural wedge made of transparent acrylic, containing a single floating fragment of silver Mylar.

    *   **The Stance:** The height of the platform and the weight of the leather skirt force a slow, deliberate stride. The stance is stoic and upright, with the deep hood forcing the head to tilt slightly forward, creating an aura of mysterious, monastic isolation.

    *   **The Palette:** A stark, high-contrast tri-tonal scheme. The Obsidian Black provides the void-like structure, the Optic White lace offers a spectral glow around the face, and the Glacial Silver "cracked" details provide the rhythmic, shattered highlights that define the "I Am Your Mirror" theme.
