Theme (condensed):
Une Lettre d’amour (A Love Letter) is a collection that reimagines the Givenchy archive through the lens of romantic correspondence and the intimacy of the written word.

Key aspects (condensed):
- The Ink Palette and Monochromatic Sentimentality: Drawing from the visual language of a manuscript, the color story is dominated by "Inkwell Black," "Parchment Cream," and "Sepia." These are punctuated by "Oxblood Red," symbolizing the wax seal that secures a private thought.
- Epistolary Tailoring: This aspect focuses on the structural translation of a letter into garment construction.
- Androgynous Correspondence: Inspired by Annemarie Schwarzenbach’s defiance of traditional gender norms, this aspect merges menswear and womenswear codes.
- Tactile Script and Materiality: The theme is physically manifested through intricate textile work that mimics the act of writing.
