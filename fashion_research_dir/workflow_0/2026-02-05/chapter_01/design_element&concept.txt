Theme: Une Lettre d’amour
Sub-Theme/Chapter: Epistolary Tailoring
==================================================
**Candidate elements/concepts**

1. Structural Stationery | The Razor-Edge Envelope Shoulder | 35%
The defining silhouette of the collection relies on an exaggerated, architectural shoulder line that mimics the sharp, unyielding corners of a heavy-stock formal envelope. This element is achieved through a bespoke internal construction involving multi-layered canvas and rigid felt padding to ensure a crisp, mathematical 90-degree angle. The aesthetic is one of disciplined elegance, echoing the 1930s androgyny of Annemarie Schwarzenbach while maintaining the couture heritage of Givenchy. These shoulders are primarily rendered in "Parchment Cream" and "Graphite Grey" bonded wools, providing a matte, opaque finish that absorbs light and emphasizes the purity of the geometric form.

1. Structural Stationery | Accordion-Fold Spine Pleating
Inspired by the expandable nature of a letter or a writer’s portfolio, this element introduces functional volume to the back of overcoats and blazers. Deep, sharp pleats are engineered to sit flush against the body when stationary but reveal a subtle depth when the wearer is in motion. The interior of the pleats may feature a contrasting "Sepia" tone in silk satin, suggesting the hidden contents of a private message. This technique allows for a slim, sharp silhouette that does not compromise on the "travel-ready" utilitarianism central to the theme's inspiration.

2. The Art of the Seal | Asymmetrical "Fold-Over" Plackets | 25%
This key design element translates the act of closing and sealing a letter into high-fashion tailoring. Jackets and waistcoats feature a double-layered front where one side folds over the other in an asymmetrical diagonal, mimicking the flap of an envelope. The closure is often hidden, maintaining a clean "manuscript" surface, but is anchored by a single, discrete point of interest. This element is frequently punctuated by a flash of "Oxblood Red" silk lining or a tonal topstitch, symbolizing the wax seal that protects the intimacy of the correspondence. The function is to create a sense of mystery and protection, wrapping the body in a structured, "sealed" garment.

2. The Art of the Seal | Tapered "Nib" Hardware
Refining the collection’s hardware, buttons and cuff fasteners are reimagined as the nibs of vintage fountain pens. Crafted from brushed ruthenium or polished silver to maintain an "Inkwell Black" and "Graphite" palette, these pieces are elongated and slightly pointed. This subtle, sculptural detail grounds the ethereal theme in tangible luxury, providing a sharp, metallic contrast to the matte textures of the "Paper-Silk" fabrics. The hardware is strategically placed on cuffs and high-collars, serving as the functional "punctuation" of the tailored look.

3. Ink & Parchment Textuality | Stiffened "Paper-Silk" Radzimir | 20%
To achieve the sensory experience of high-quality stationery, we introduce a custom-developed "Paper-Silk" Radzimir. This material possesses a high-twist yarn density that creates a dry, almost crunchy hand-feel, mimicking the tactile quality of vellum or parchment. The fabric is treated with a light resin coating to hold a permanent, knife-edge crease, essential for the "Epistolary Tailoring" sub-theme. Rendered in a deep, saturated "Inkwell Black," this material reflects a soft, intellectual sheen that suggests the depth of freshly dried ink on a page, providing a sophisticated bridge between evening-wear elegance and structural precision.

3. Ink & Parchment Textuality | Layered Organza "Vellum" Overlays
This element utilizes semi-transparent, stiffened silk organza to create a "Vellum" effect over more structured base layers. By layering varying tones of "Parchment Cream" and "Sepia," the garments gain a dimensional transparency that suggests the honesty and vulnerability of a written confession. These overlays are often used as sheer panels on the sides of trousers or as detachable "dust jackets" for blazers, blurring the lines between the rigid exterior of the envelope and the delicate, transparent nature of the thoughts contained within.

4. The Androgynous Script | High-Rise "Knife-Edge" Trousers | 20%
Rooted in the 1930s travel aesthetic, these trousers feature an extreme high-waist and a wide, fluid leg that maintains a sharp, vertical crease. The "Knife-Edge" is achieved through a permanent silicone-bead pressing technique on the interior of the fabric, ensuring the crease never softens. The silhouette is distinctly androgynous, offering a commanding presence that honors Givenchy’s history of dressing independent, self-assured women. These trousers are primarily presented in "Graphite Grey" and "Parchment Cream," serving as the foundational "blank page" upon which the more complex tailored jackets are layered.
