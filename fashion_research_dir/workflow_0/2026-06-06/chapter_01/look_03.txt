Look 3: Shoreline Modernism

1. The Outerwear: Cropped Nautical Jacket in Chalk White Gabardine
A sharply tailored cropped jacket sits close to the shoulders and skims the upper torso with a crisp, buoyant ease, bringing the salon’s discipline into daylight. Cut in chalk white lightweight gabardine, it has a clean boxy shape with softly rounded corners at the hem, allowing the body to move without stiffness. The shoulders are lightly structured for presence, but the jacket remains airy and wearable, opening neatly over the layer beneath so the look feels ready for the shore rather than trapped in formality. Subtle navy edge topstitching traces the seams like a graphic frame, while polished black buttons provide that precise Chanel contrast and keep the silhouette anchored.
    *   Key Structure: cropped shoulder line / single-breasted closure / straight short length / slightly eased body
    *   Material & Finish: lightweight cotton gabardine with a matte surface / polished black buttons / navy topstitching

2. The Foundation: Marinière Knit Top and Straight Shorts Set
Beneath the jacket, a fine marinière knit in navy and ivory introduces the collection’s sailor rhythm with a clean, compact fit that lies smoothly against the body. The knit is slim but not tight, finishing at the waist so it can meet the shorts with clarity and avoid bulk under the cropped outer layer. The shorts are cut straight and tidy in deep navy cotton canvas, sitting just above the knee to keep the line youthful, mobile, and seaworthy. Their precise front creases and flat waistband create a calm vertical fall, balancing the jacket’s sharpness while preserving a sense of freedom and promenade-ready movement.
    *   Top: fine navy-and-ivory striped knit with a neat waist-length finish
    *   Bottom: straight navy cotton-canvas shorts with clean front pleats
    *   Fit & Line: close at the torso, easy through the leg, and balanced by a crisp horizontal hem

3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware
The look is sharpened by restrained hardware and disciplined finishing: small black lacquered buttons, a slim dark leather belt, and a neat edge of navy binding at the jacket front. The belt defines the waist without interrupting the clean geometry, its subtle sheen echoing the polished buttons and creating a quiet rhythm of black accents against the pale jacket and striped knit. A faint sand-beige lining glimpses at the jacket edge when it moves, adding a warm coastal softness that keeps the ensemble from feeling severe. These details work as structure rather than decoration, translating Chanel’s graphic identity into usable, modern closure points.
    *   Hardware Focus: glossy black buttons and a discreet blackened buckle
    *   Waist Treatment: slim black leather belt cinching the jacket over the striped knit

4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance
Flat black leather sandals complete the silhouette with a low, grounded stance that feels practical for the promenade yet refined enough for a salon setting. The sandals are slim and minimal, with narrow straps and a lightly cushioned sole that keeps the line clean from leg to ground, leaving the cropped shorts and tailored jacket fully visible. Their matte leather finish keeps the look disciplined, while the open construction introduces the air and movement central to the shoreline theme. Worn with an assured, forward stride, the footwear gives the entire outfit its sense of ease, as if the wearer can cross from terrace to sand without changing pace.
    *   Shoe: flat black leather sandals with slim straps and a low-profile sole
