Theme (condensed):
Sous le Salon la Plage is a Chanel Cruise narrative of origin and release: a return to Biarritz, where the house’s modern identity first took shape, and a reimagining of salon codes as they flow toward the sea.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Heritage as living origin: The collection is anchored in Biarritz not as a nostalgic backdrop but as Chanel’s first design laboratory for modernity.
- Freedom as bodily movement: The theme centers on the body in motion—dressing, undressing, swimming, walking, and crossing settings without costume change.
- Rebellion refined into classics: Blazy emphasizes that Chanel’s classics were originally disruptive.
- Salon-to-beach duality: A core tension runs through the collection between the codes of interior elegance and outdoor freedom.
- Workwear re-authored as luxury: Marinière stripes, bleu de travail references, and washed canvas tailoring shift class associations while retaining practical credibility.
- Coastal graphic identity: Basque stripes, marine shimmer, shell references, and seaside color associations create a strong visual vocabulary that is not literal resort dressing.
- Craft, structure, and surface tension: The collection’s materials—tweed, raffia, silk, beaded knitwear, sequins, canvas—create a dialogue between softness and precision.
- Accessories as passage tools: Bags, shoes, and jewelry are conceived as instruments for moving across environments and identities.
