A study in sartorial duality, this look juxtaposes rigid masculine tailoring with high-glint fractured surfaces, embodying the "I Am Your Mirror" concept through a split-axis construction. The ensemble balances the weight of traditional wool against the sharp, reflective brilliance of mirrored glass, creating a silhouette that is both grounded and ephemeral.

Look 3: L'Écho de l’Autre (The Echo of the Other)

1. The Outerwear: The "Garçonne" Mirror Blazer
This double-breasted blazer is bisected down the center seam to represent the duality of the public and private self. The right side is crafted from light-absorbing Obsidian Black wool barathea, while the left is entirely encrusted with a mosaic of hand-applied, micro-mirrored Chrome shards. The internal canvas is reinforced with multi-layered horsehair to perfectly balance the disparate weights of the matte wool and the heavy mirrored embellishment.
    *   Key Structure: Padded 1930s shoulders / peak lapel / double-breasted / hip-length
    *   Material & Finish: Matte wool barathea + mirrored acrylic shards + gunmetal internal buttons

2. The Foundation: High-Waisted "Envelope" Trousers
These wide-leg trousers provide a fluid, nomadic base in Ink-Wash Grey wool crepe, featuring a waist construction that mimics a folded stationery set. The fabric overlaps at the navel in a sharp V-shape, concealing a silver zip, while polished Chrome patent leather side-stripes run down the outer seams to elongate the leg.
    *   Top: Hidden sleeveless bodysuit in Mercury Silver silk-jersey
    *   Bottom: Wide-leg Ink-Wash Grey wool crepe trousers
    *   Fit & Line: Architectural high-waisted fit with a flowing, floor-grazing hem

3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware
Functional elements are reimagined as epistolary artifacts, with all closures featuring heavy, gunmetal-toned silver hardware cast to resemble 19th-century wax seals. The underside of the blazer’s notched lapel is lined in silk habotai printed with microscopic excerpts of Hubert de Givenchy’s archival correspondence, visible only when the collar is turned.
    *   Hardware Focus: 4G-embossed wax-seal buttons in tarnished gunmetal
    *   Waist Treatment: Geometric "envelope" fold with hidden silver-tooth fastener

4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance
The model wears a pointed-toe Chelsea boot in Mercury Silver metallic leather, featuring a sharp stiletto heel that echoes the "fractured light" motif. The stance is assertive and androgynous, with movements intended to catch the high-intensity spotlights, sending splinters of reflected light across the obsidian runway.
    *   Shoe: Pointed-toe metallic leather boot in Mercury Silver

5. The Palette
Primary: Obsidian Black; blazer right side and accents; 40%
Secondary: Ink-Wash Grey; trousers; 35%
Accent: Chrome and Mercury Silver; blazer left side, side-stripes, and hardware; 25%
