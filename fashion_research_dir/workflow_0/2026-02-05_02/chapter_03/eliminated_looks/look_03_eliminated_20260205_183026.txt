Look 3: The Dualist’s Epistolary Silhouette

1. The Outerwear: The "Garçonne" Mirror Blazer
This structural centerpiece explores the "I Am Your Mirror" concept through a stark, symmetrical duality split down the center vertical axis. The left side is masterfully tailored from heavy, matte Obsidian Black wool barathea, while the right side is entirely encrusted with hand-applied micro-mirrored shards that create a fractured, high-glint surface. The construction features sharp, padded 1930s shoulders and a double-breasted closure, utilizing an internal reinforced canvas to balance the disparate weights of the matte fabric and the metallic embellishment.
    *   Key Structure: Padded 1930s shoulders/double-breasted/split-axis construction/hip-length
    *   Material & Finish: Matte wool barathea + micro-mirrored glass shards + Chrome hardware

2. The Foundation: The Liquid Letter Set
A fluid, bias-cut top in 40mm Mercury Silver silk satin provides a molten contrast to the rigid blazer, appearing to pour off the torso like spilled metallic ink. It is paired with high-waisted "Envelope" Trousers in Ink-Wash Grey wool crepe, featuring a wide-leg silhouette and a geometric V-shape fold at the waistband that mimics the closure of a stationery set. The trousers are elongated by high-shine Chrome patent leather side-stripes that provide a sharp, masculine verticality.
    *   Top: Mercury Silver molten silk with an structured envelope neckline
    *   Bottom: Ink-Wash Grey wide-leg trousers with Chrome patent side-stripes
    *   Fit & Line: Architectural waist with fluid, wide-leg movement

3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware
The garment's functional elements are reimagined as epistolary symbols, specifically the "Wax-Seal" hardware cast in tarnished gunmetal silver and embossed with the 4G logo on the blazer cuffs. The internal lining of the blazer features microscopic prints of archival Givenchy correspondence on silk habotai, visible only when the garment moves.
    *   Hardware Focus: Gunmetal tarnished silver wax-seal buttons
    *   Waist Treatment: Overlapping geometric "envelope" fold with hidden silver zip

4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance
The look is anchored by square-toed boots in Obsidian Black patent leather, featuring a cylindrical Chrome metallic heel that reflects the runway's black obsidian floor. The stance is rigid and androgynous, emphasizing the broad shoulders and the sharp contrast between the light-absorbing wool and the light-reflecting mirror shards.
    *   Shoe: Obsidian patent leather boot with Chrome metallic heel

5. The Palette
Primary: Obsidian Black; blazer left-half and footwear; 45%
Secondary: Ink-Wash Grey; wide-leg trousers; 30%
Accent: Chrome and Mercury Silver; mirror embroidery, silk top, and hardware; 25%
