Look 3: The Secretive Correspondent

1. The Outerwear: Envelope-Fold Trench Coat
This mid-length trench is meticulously constructed from heavy Bone White wool gabardine, featuring a radical asymmetrical front closure that mimics a sealed envelope. The right side of the chest integrates a high-stiffened "collar-flap" that stands rigid against the neck, while the interior is fully lined in Dried Rose silk habotai embroidered with tonal poetic excerpts.
    *   Key Structure: High-stiffened stand collar; asymmetrical wrap closure; crisp architectonic shoulders; mid-thigh length.
    *   Material & Finish: Heavy wool gabardine with a matte, paper-like finish and hidden silk habotai lining.

2. The Foundation: Deconstructed Bettina Blouse & Ink-Bleed Skirt
The core set pairs a crisp Ivory cotton poplin blouse with a structural midi-skirt. The blouse features raw-edged, layered ruffles on the cuffs and placket that resemble the stacked, weathered pages of a journal, while the high-waisted skirt utilizes a calligraphic ink-bleed jacquard to create a blurred, ombré effect.
    *   Top: Ivory paper-touch cotton blouse with frayed-edge organza "page" ruffles.
    *   Bottom: Deep Charcoal and Bone White ink-bleed jacquard midi-skirt with an "envelope" fold-over waist.
    *   Fit & Line: Sharp, narrow shoulders transitioning into a structured, pencil-line lower silhouette.

3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware
Functional hardware is reimagined as "Sceau du Secret" wax seals, cast in matte Molten Gold with irregular edges and an embossed 4G logo. These seals secure the asymmetrical waist flap of the skirt and the cuffs of the outerwear, providing a heavy, tactile contrast to the fluid "ink" patterns of the textile.
    *   Hardware Focus: Molten Gold "wax seal" buttons and sculptural waist clasps.
    *   Waist Treatment: Origami-style fold-over waistband secured by a signature gold seal.

4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance
The look is grounded by sharp-toed ankle boots in Deep Charcoal calfskin, featuring a sculptural gold inset in the heel. The model’s stance is poised and guarded, reflecting the "nomadic aristocrat" who carries her secrets within the folds of her garments, moving with a deliberate, rhythmic grace.
    *   Shoe: Pointed-toe calfskin ankle boot with a matte charcoal finish.

5. The Palette
Primary: Bone White; Trench and Blouse base; 60%
Secondary: Deep Charcoal; Jacquard skirt and Footwear; 30%
Accent: Dried Rose and Molten Gold; Hidden linings and Hardware; 10%
