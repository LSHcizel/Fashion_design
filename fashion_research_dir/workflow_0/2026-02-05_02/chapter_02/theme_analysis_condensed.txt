Theme (condensed):
"Une Lettre d’amour" (A Love Letter) serves as a profound meditation on the enduring legacy of Givenchy, reimagined as an intimate correspondence between the house’s aristocratic foundations and a contemporary, fluid sensibility.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Sartorial Epistolary Details: Embodying the "Lettre d’amour" motif, the garments incorporate hidden details that mimic the intimacy of a private letter.
- The Androgynous Silhouette: Central to the collection is the tension between traditional masculine tailoring and ethereal femininity.
- The Mirror and Reflection: Drawing from the concept "I Am Your Mirror," the collection utilizes reflective textures and symmetrical design structures to explore the theme of duality.
- Aristocratic Nomadism: Blending Givenchy’s noble heritage with Schwarzenbach’s life of travel, this aspect focuses on "functional elegance." It takes the high-society glamour of the 1950s—such as the iconic Bettina blouse—and recontextualizes it for a modern wanderer.
