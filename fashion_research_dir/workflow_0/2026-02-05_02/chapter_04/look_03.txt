Look 3: The Horizon’s Envoy

1. The Outerwear: The "Great-Envelope" Duster
An elongated, floor-sweeping duster coat that exemplifies the "Enveloped" silhouette, providing a protective yet architectural layer for the aristocratic nomad. The garment is constructed from Basalt Black weather-treated bonded cashmere, giving it a monolithic, sculptural weight that resists the elements while maintaining a soft, matte finish. The interior is fully lined in Dusty Parchment silk-gabardine, featuring tonal micro-embroidered poetry that remains a private luxury for the wearer.
    *   Key Structure: Standing origami-fold lapel / hidden double-breasted closure / floor-length / raglan sleeve
    *   Material & Finish: Bonded cashmere + silk-gabardine + matte weather-resistant membrane

2. The Foundation: The Nomad-Bettina Set
A high-contrast pairing that balances mid-century volume with rugged utility, consisting of a deconstructed blouse and shimmering wide-leg trousers. The blouse features oversized, sun-bleached Antique Khaki ruffles that are intentionally frayed at the edges to suggest a storied history, while the trousers provide a fluid, reflective base that catches the sunset.
    *   Top: Nomad-Bettina blouse in Antique Khaki silk-poplin with adjustable weather-treated suede sleeve straps.
    *   Bottom: Cargo-Couture high-waisted trousers in Sun-Bleached Metallic-Linen Weave with hidden-pleat bellows pockets.
    *   Fit & Line: Sharp, cinched waist transitioning into voluminous, fluid proportions.

3. The Details: Materiality & Hardware
The look is anchored by functional artifacts, most notably the modular "Journal" harness in Burnt Sienna vegetable-tanned leather that cinches the waist and provides attachment points for travel essentials. Hardware is heavy and historical, utilizing the signature wax seal motifs to secure the heavy cashmere and silk layers.
    *   Hardware Focus: Oxidized Gold "Wax Seal" fasteners debossed with the Givenchy emblem.
    *   Waist Treatment: Burnt Sienna leather modular strapping with recessed metallic clasps.

4. The Finish: Footwear & Stance
The model moves with a cinematic, purposeful stride, the heavy hem of the duster coat trailing slightly to emphasize the "Golden Hour" atmosphere. The footwear is a refined interpretation of a traveler's boot, providing a sturdy foundation for the expansive silhouette.
    *   Shoe: Square-toed block-heel nomad boots in Basalt Black calfskin with Oxidized Gold heel plating.

5. The Palette
Primary: Basalt Black / Outerwear and Footwear / 50%
Secondary: Dusty Parchment & Antique Khaki / Lapels, Blouse, and Trousers / 40%
Accent: Oxidized Gold & Burnt Sienna / Hardware, Weave, and Leather Harness / 10%
