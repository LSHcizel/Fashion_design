**Theme:**
"Une Lettre d’amour" (A Love Letter) serves as a profound meditation on the enduring legacy of Givenchy, reimagined as an intimate correspondence between the house’s aristocratic foundations and a contemporary, fluid sensibility. This collection is envisioned as a romantic dialogue that transcends time, weaving together the structural purity of Hubert de Givenchy’s mid-century couture with a modern, emotive vulnerability. It explores the concept of the garment as a vessel for personal history—an epistolary expression of elegance that is both a tribute to the past and a declaration of a new, inclusive future for the maison.

**Inspiration:**
The collection draws its primary creative spark from the enigmatic figure of Annemarie Schwarzenbach, a Swiss writer, photographer, and traveler of the 1930s whose androgynous beauty and restless spirit mirror the "I Am Your Mirror" philosophy. The designer translates Schwarzenbach’s penchant for masculine tailoring and nomadic functionality into the Givenchy lexicon, bridging the gap between the gamine charm of Audrey Hepburn and a more subversive, gender-defying sophistication. By viewing Schwarzenbach through the lens of a "love letter," the collection treats her aesthetic as a reflection of the wearer’s own internal complexity, using "I Am Your Mirror" to signify how fashion acts as a reflective surface for identity, memory, and desire.

**Key aspects of the theme:**

**The Androgynous Silhouette:**
Central to the collection is the tension between traditional masculine tailoring and ethereal femininity. Inspired by Schwarzenbach’s "garçonne" style, the collection features oversized trench coats, sharp-shouldered blazers, and high-waisted trousers that coexist with delicate silk slips and lace overlays. This duality pays homage to Givenchy’s history of dressing women who possessed a certain boyish insouciance, evolving the brand’s DNA into a space where gender lines are blurred by the shared language of impeccable construction and refined fabrics.

**Sartorial Epistolary Details:**
Embodying the "Lettre d’amour" motif, the garments incorporate hidden details that mimic the intimacy of a private letter. This includes embroidered excerpts of poetry on inner linings, hardware that resembles vintage wax seals, and "envelope" folds in the draping of skirts and bodices. These elements suggest a hidden narrative, where the luxury of the piece is not just visible to the world but felt privately by the wearer, reinforcing the idea of the garment as a personal, cherished correspondence.

**The Mirror and Reflection:**
Drawing from the concept "I Am Your Mirror," the collection utilizes reflective textures and symmetrical design structures to explore the theme of duality. High-shine satins, metallic threading, and polished hardware serve as literal reflections, while mirrored embroidery patterns create a visual dialogue across the body. This aspect emphasizes the psychological relationship between the individual and their image, suggesting that the clothes we wear are both a reflection of who we are and an idealization of who we wish to be.

**Aristocratic Nomadism:**
Blending Givenchy’s noble heritage with Schwarzenbach’s life of travel, this aspect focuses on "functional elegance." It takes the high-society glamour of the 1950s—such as the iconic Bettina blouse—and recontextualizes it for a modern wanderer. Utilitarian pockets, durable wools, and modular pieces are elevated through couture-level finishing, representing a love letter to a life lived in motion. It captures the spirit of a person who is as comfortable in a Parisian salon as they are exploring the rugged landscapes of the Silk Road.
