Theme (condensed):
Une Lettre d’amour reimagines Givenchy as a collection suspended between intimacy and elegance, where fashion becomes a personal correspondence written in fabric, silhouette, and gesture.

Key aspects (condensed):
- Emotional intimacy: The collection should capture the private emotionality of a love letter, translating feeling into quiet luxury.
- Givenchy elegance and restraint: Givenchy’s legacy provides a framework of precision, polish, and controlled sophistication.
- Romantic duality: A love letter inherently contains longing, anticipation, memory, and confession, and this duality should be expressed through contrasts in the collection.
- Literary and handwritten symbolism: As a theme, Une Lettre d’amour invites direct references to writing, correspondence, and the tactile ritual of letter-making.
- Annemarie Schwarzenbach’s poetic androgyny: Schwarzenbach’s image introduces a compelling cultural layer of androgynous beauty, travel, fragility, and distance.
- Palette of memory and affection: The color story should support the emotional tone of a love letter through muted, romantic, and ink-like shades.
- Tactility and material depth: Material choices should emphasize touch, since a letter is an intimate object meant to be held.
- Movement and suspended emotion: The collection should suggest the physicality of receiving, reading, and folding a letter—moments of pause, breath, and anticipation.
