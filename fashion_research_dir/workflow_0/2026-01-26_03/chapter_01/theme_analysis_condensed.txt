Theme (condensed):
"I Am Your Mirror" serves as a profound exploration of identity, duality, and the symbiotic relationship between the observer and the observed within the house of Givenchy.

Key aspects (condensed):
- The Androgynous Silhouette: Central to the theme is the erasure of rigid gender binaries through precise tailoring.
- Reflective Materiality and Liquid Surfaces: To visually manifest the "mirror" theme, the collection employs a palette of high-sheen fabrics, including silk lamé, polished leather, and technical satins that mimic the surface of still water.
- Symmetry and Optical Illusion: The construction of the garments leans heavily on Rorschach-like symmetry and trompe-l'œil details.
- The Nomadic Aristocrat: Blending the utilitarian needs of a world traveler with the refinement of Parisian haute couture, this aspect focuses on functionality hidden within luxury.
- The Introspective Gaze: Beyond the physical, the theme addresses the cultural significance of how we view ourselves in the digital age.
